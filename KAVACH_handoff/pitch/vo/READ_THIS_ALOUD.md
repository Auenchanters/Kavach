# Reference recording for the KAVACH narrator voice

Who: one teammate who agrees to their voice being cloned for this pitch video.
Where: a quiet room, phone 20-30 cm from the mouth, no music or fan noise.
How: read the paragraph below once, calmly, at the pace you would present. 20-30 seconds is perfect.
Save as: `pitch/vo/ref_voice.m4a` (phone voice memo), `.mp3` or `.wav`, all work.

---

Every day, scammers call our parents pretending to be the police, the bank, or a courier company.
KAVACH sits on that call with them. It listens, warns them calmly in Kannada, Hindi or English,
and tells their son or daughter on Telegram. If money is lost, it races the golden hour:
it alerts the bank, prepares the 1930 helpline briefing, and reports the number to Sanchar Saathi.
No Amma should ever be left alone with a scammer.
